# Company data

**Settings → Company data** (`/admin/settings/company`)

Your business identity, used in the storefront (header logo, footer), emails and the order summary. All fields are
optional.

| Tab | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Company** | Company name, company phone and mobile, website, online store opening year |
| **Address** | Street address, city, country, region / state, postal code |
| **Tax details** | Legal business name (if different from the store name), business activity, tax identification number (for Greek companies the 9-digit ΑΦΜ), tax office, GEMI registration number |
| **Contact emails** | Customer enquiries, store administrator, order enquiries, help and support, newsletter reply |
| **Social & apps** | Links to your social media pages (drag to reorder, default or custom icons) and to your mobile apps (built-in badges or your own) |
| **Logo** | **Store logo** (all devices), optional **Mobile logo** (phones), optional **Email logo**, and the **Favicon** (browser tab icon). PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB |
| **Payment icons** | Pictures of the payment methods you accept, shown in the footer |

Logos are previewed on a light and a dark background. The email logo is used in emails; without it the store logo is
used, and without any logo the store name.

{: .note }
> The sender address of order emails is not set here but in **Settings → General settings → Emails**.

## Permission

*Manage general and company settings* (`settings.manage`).
