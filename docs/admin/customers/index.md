# Customers

| Screen | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [All customers](customers/) | Customer profiles, addresses, online accounts, invitations, tax exemption, notes |
| [Customer groups](customer-groups/) | Segments such as *Retail*, *VIP* or *Wholesale*, used by prices, promotions and visibility rules |
| [Blacklist](blacklist/) | Email addresses and phone numbers that are flagged or that may not use certain payment methods |

**Customers and staff are separate.** A customer profile never gives access to the administration, and staff accounts
are managed in [Users](../users-and-roles/).
