# Blog

**Marketing → Blog → Blogs** (`/admin/blogs`) and **Marketing → Blog → Blog posts** (`/admin/blog-posts`)

A store can have several **blogs** (for example *News* and *Guides*). Each blog has its own page listing its published
posts, and every **post** belongs to one blog.

## Blogs

Click **Create blog** and enter, per language, the **Name**, a **Description** and the SEO fields (SEO name, title,
description). Choose the status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Listed and reachable |
| **Hidden** | Reachable by its address only |
| **Disabled** | Not shown, and neither are its posts |

A blog with posts cannot be deleted until its posts are deleted or moved.

## Posts

Click **Create post** (create a blog first) and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Blog** | The blog the post belongs to |
| **Title**, **Content**, **Excerpt** | Per language. The excerpt is the short summary in the blog's post list |
| **Publication date** | Empty keeps the post a **draft**; a future date schedules it |
| **Cover image** | Shown in the post list and at the top of the post, with alternative text |
| **Mobile cover** | Optional, for example a portrait crop shown on phones |
| **Tags** | Separated by commas |
| **Status** | Active (listed on its blog), Hidden (reachable by its address only), Disabled |

A post appears on its blog once it is **published, active and available**.

## Writing with AI

With an AI key configured and the *Write blog posts with AI* permission, the assistant drafts posts in every store
language as a chat inside the editor. You review and save.

## Linking posts to categories

In a category's editor you can choose **Blog posts** to show on the category page, above its questions, in the order
you set. Only published, active posts appear.

## Bulk actions

Tick blogs or posts in the lists to change or delete several at once.

## Permissions

*View blogs*, *Manage blogs and posts*, *Write blog posts with AI*.
