# Reels

**Marketing → Reels** (`/admin/reels`)

Reels are short videos uploaded by the store, with products linked to them. Shoppers open a reel from a reels block on
the storefront, watch it and see the linked products with live prices next to the video (below it on phones).

## Creating a reel

Click **Create** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Per language; required in the default language |
| **Video** | One MP4 file, up to 64 MB. H.264 video with AAC audio plays in the most browsers |
| **Cover image** | Required. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB |
| **Products** | Up to 24 products, in order (optional) |
| **Status** | Active or Disabled |

The store does not convert videos or extract covers automatically.

## Showing reels on the storefront

In **Website → Layouts**, add a **Reels** block to a grid: choose reels by hand, or automatically by **Newest reels**
or **Most viewed reels** within a period (set in **Settings → General settings → Catalog**, 90 days by default). A
single reel fills its grid; a carousel shows thumbnails. Disabled reels and reels without a default-language title or
media are skipped.

## Views and history

A view is counted after three seconds of visible playback, at most once per viewer per half hour. Staff with *View reel
viewers and viewing history* can see the history of a reel. No IP address or device fingerprint is stored; these are
playback signals, not exact audience measurement.

## Permissions

*View reels*, *Manage reels*, *View reel viewers and viewing history*.
