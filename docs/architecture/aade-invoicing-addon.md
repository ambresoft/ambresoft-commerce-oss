# Greek fiscal invoicing and the future AADE addon

Status: **deferred implementation, explicitly requested as an optional addon**. Research checked on 24 October 2026. The commercial document is now labelled **Order summary / Σύνοψη παραγγελίας**. The current work improves that document and its editor; it does not implement fiscal issuance or AADE transmission.

## Current document and its limits

The order-summary title, email labels, print action and customer download use commercial-summary wording in all six shipped languages. Migration 2026_11_10_000200 renames generated subjects and standalone legacy headings without replacing merchant copy or styling. Existing `/invoice` URLs, template keys and notification-kind IDs remain compatible. The future official AADE receipt/invoice is a separate artifact. Retail and business document selection must be determined by the actual transaction and required fiscal identities, not merely a title or account role; see [AADE sales-document guidance](https://www.aade.gr/exypiretisi-enimerosi/hristikoi-odigoi/enarxi-epiheirimatikis-drastiriotitas/ekdidomena-stoiheia-apo-tin-epiheirisi-parastatika).

The existing six-column invoice design remains the default. Orders → Email templates → Order summary exposes the full document HTML, a repeating product-row template and a repeating totals-row template per language. `{{invoice_items}}` and `{{invoice_totals}}` place the generated rows. Known `{{item_*}}` values work inside the product row; `{{invoice_total_label}}` and `{{invoice_total_value}}` inside the totals row. Money, tax, discount allocation and payment calculations remain server-owned. Templates are sanitized HTML, never PHP/Blade or executable expressions. Legacy `{{invoice}}` bodies expand in the editor without overwriting saved content merely on opening it.

Product sales already included in the charged unit price are shown as informational reductions, with effective percentages and net amounts. Allocated coupon/promotion discounts reduce the line's net value before VAT. Their names/codes in totals identify reductions already included, rather than deducting them twice. Captured gift-card redemptions are shown after the gross total as settlement, separately from discounts. They leave the order's taxable sale unchanged. The remaining amount due includes other captured payments too.

Verified customers can download printable HTML from their own order list/detail after the first recorded invoice-status notification. This is independent of email delivery success and remains available after subsequent status changes. Other customers receive 404. The current download is freshly rendered using the saved order and current template; it is **not an immutable issued fiscal document or a PDF**. Admin print, email and account download use the same template and logo selection (email logo, then store logo).

The CMS currently lacks fiscal series/number allocation, a separate issue/supply date, complete seller/buyer fiscal identity capture, immutable issuance records, credit-note issuance, official transmission results and a fiscal document archive. Historical per-line tax rates are incomplete before the new pricing snapshots. Complex taxes, exemptions and voucher issuance require additional fiscal modeling. Editing this HTML cannot supply those guarantees. The merchant must continue using its appropriate lawful issuing channel until that work is implemented and verified with its accountant.

## Legal findings informing the work

- [Law 4308/2014, original Gazette, Article 9](https://api.et.gr/apiLAW/1/2014/4308/pdf): fiscal identity, unique sequential invoice number, issue/supply dates as applicable, quantities, net unit values, values by VAT rate, VAT rates/amounts and applicable special statements are required. Discount values must appear when not already included in unit prices. This is not a blanket requirement to print a percentage for every discount. Article 8 addresses credit invoices for subsequent adjustments. Check amendments when implementing issuance.
- [AADE VAT Code consolidation, Articles 16 and 24](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2026-04/n.5144_2024.pdf): qualifying price reductions reduce taxable consideration. Single-purpose and multi-purpose vouchers have different VAT timing. The name “gift card” alone does not determine the category. Record the voucher's actual consideration and applicable treatment; third-party reimbursement needs separate review. Do not tax the same supply again solely because a stored-value payment is redeemed.
- [European Commission invoicing guidance](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-businesses/invoicing_en) distinguishes invoice obligations and applicable national rules.
- [AADE electronic invoicing FAQs](https://www.aade.gr/ypohreotiki-ilektroniki-timologisi-psifiaka-parastatika-diakinisis-syhnes-erotiseis) describe available issuing channels and phased obligations. A successful myDATA submission alone must not be presented as proof that every business has met its electronic-issuance obligations. Confirm the merchant's permitted channel and applicability at implementation time.

## Proposed addon boundary (not yet published contracts)

Use the existing [contract-2 addon lifecycle](module-system.md) and context boundaries. The optional Greek AADE adapter owns credentials, XML/XSD mappings, transmission/reconciliation, official references and its integration settings. Core continues owning orders, price/tax snapshots, payment settlement and customer ownership checks. Core must not import the addon or require it to sell products.

At implementation time, introduce only the consumed, framework-free Order/Application fiscal-document contracts: a complete versioned issuance snapshot, a request to issue/correct, and an authorized document retrieval projection. The addon calls these published operations; it must not call controllers or modify order tables directly. No speculative interface, permission, module folder or event bus is introduced now.

Fiscal identity and immutable document facts must be modeled separately from mutable order and template data. Required prerequisites include seller/buyer tax identity and branch/address, document type and series, issuance/supply dates, currency, line quantities/unit net amounts, explicit net discount allocations, taxable bases/VAT amounts per category, exemptions/reasons, ancillary charges, totals and payment/voucher classification. An order number is only a reference, never the fiscal number. Do not invent missing identifiers or infer historical tax facts from today's catalog.

## Integration plan and acceptance gates

1. Select the lawful issuing channel with the merchant/accountant: ERP REST transmission, a licensed provider, or another supported AADE channel. AADE's ERP REST API is not a license to operate as an electronic-invoicing provider. Pin the applicable schema and API version from [AADE technical specifications](https://www.aade.gr/mydata/tehnikes-prodiagrafes-ekdoseis-mydata) when implementing (the portal currently lists 2.0.2); use its official ERP/provider documents and XSDs. Recheck versions and rules before production.
2. Add explicit, separately authorized fiscal configuration/issue/retry/correction actions, super-admin-only initially and delegable through persisted permissions. Encrypt credentials, separate test/production environments, redact secrets/PII from operational logs, bound requests/timeouts and protect official document downloads with the same owner check as orders. Provider boot registers services only; no migrations, network calls or issuance.
3. Validate a complete snapshot and reconcile decimal amounts per VAT category before accepting issuance. Include mixed rates, tax-inclusive/exclusive prices, full/partial discounts, shipping/payment fees, rounding, exemptions and currency rules. Require accountant-approved SPV/MPV mappings and discounted/third-party-funded voucher cases. Templates cannot remove required fiscal facts from the issued artifact.
4. Reserve the fiscal series/number exactly once under a transaction lock with unique constraints. Persist the issuance request and an idempotent outbox entry atomically; contact AADE only after commit. A status/email event can request an issuance workflow, but must never pretend issuance succeeded. Separate queued/submitted/accepted/rejected/uncertain outcomes from email-delivery states. After timeouts reconcile before retrying, preventing duplicate documents.
5. Store responses and the applicable returned identifiers (MARK/UID/authentication/QR where required by that channel/type) against the immutable snapshot. Preserve accepted documents, their template/version and the exact customer artifact, with a hash and access audit. An issued document must not change when an order, address, brand logo or template changes. Corrections use the legally appropriate linked credit/cancellation/replacement workflow, never an overwrite or delete.
6. Once official issuance is in use, email and account download must deliver the retained official artifact and references. Pending/rejected issuance must never offer a draft as an issued invoice. Preserve the present visual design where lawful, while adding the required fiscal content. Generate/archive an official PDF or use the issuing provider's artifact according to the selected channel.
7. Exercise sandbox acceptance, schema validation, rate/category mappings, rejects, timeouts, duplicate/reordered jobs, retries after process crashes, corrections, restricted access and backup restoration. Obtain merchant/accountant sign-off before enabling live issuance. Disabling/uninstalling the addon stops new submissions without deleting fiscal records; retained documents need a supported authorized read path and statutory retention policy.

No AADE REST request, credential field, fiscal numbering or fiscal PDF generator is included in the current change.
